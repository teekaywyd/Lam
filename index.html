About

Six years old, and growing on purpose.

Lambano Academy opened its doors in 2020. What hasn't changed since is the idea we built it on.

Our history

Founded on one idea.

Lambano Academy opened in January 2020 in Sandton with 90 students spread across every grade, from Grade R to matric. The founders' idea was plain: keep the school small enough that every teacher knows every student's name, at every age — even while enrolment grew.

Today we're 520 students on the same Sandton campus, with a second block added in 2023 to keep pace — built to match the original in look and feel.

Mission & vision

What we're here to do.

Our Mission

Nurture every learner, know every name.

To provide every student, from Grade R to matric, with a values-driven education inside classrooms small enough that no child goes unseen — equipping them academically, socially, and morally to thrive beyond Lambano's gates.

Our Vision

Confident, capable South Africans.

To be recognised as one of South Africa's leading independent schools — producing graduates who are university-ready, community-minded, and equipped to lead with integrity in a changing world.

FAQs

Common questions.

Do you accept transfer students mid-year?+

Yes, on a space-available basis. Reach out to admissions and we'll confirm openings for the current term.

Is there before- and after-school care?+

Care runs from 07:00 until 17:30 for grades Grade R–8, included in tuition for the lower grades.

What's the school's uniform policy?+

Uniforms are required Grade R–8; the upper school follows a dress code rather than a uniform.

Academics

A shared start, widening into real choice.

The same foundation for every student in the early grades, then a genuine track of IEB subject choices by Grade 11.

By grade band

What students take, and when.

Grade R–5

Foundations

Reading, number sense, and a first language class, taught mostly through projects rather than worksheets.

6–8

Middle studios

Students rotate through science, design, and humanities studios instead of siloed subjects.

9–12

IEB & university-prep track

18 IEB and university-prep subjects across sciences, humanities, and languages, with exam prep built into the syllabus.

12

Senior capstone

A year-long independent research project, defended in front of a faculty panel before graduation.

Outcomes

Where seniors go next.

Admissions

How to apply, and what we look for.

We admit across all grades, Grade R through 11, with most openings at Grade R and grade 9.

The process

Four steps, roughly eight weeks.

01

Inquire

Fill out the short form on our contact page, or call the admissions office directly.

02

Tour & shadow day

Visit campus on a Tuesday or Thursday. Applicants for grades 6 and up shadow a current student for a day.

03

Submit the application

Transcripts, two teacher recommendations, and a short student essay — no standardized test required below grade 9.

04

Decision

Families hear back within four weeks of a completed application.

Tuition & aid

Cost, plainly stated.

Tuition varies by grade band; roughly a third of families receive some form of need-based aid.

Grade R–5

R64,500 / year

Includes materials, lunch, and aftercare through 17:30.

Grade 6–8

R78,900 / year

Includes materials and access to all clubs and middle school teams.

Grade 9–12

R92,400 / year

Includes IEB exam fees and one-on-one university counselling from Grade 11.
